Filmi Paheli Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Filmi Paheli ("the app") is a Bollywood guess-the-film game for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses and what it does not.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not run our own servers that store information about you.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress (solved films, coins, streak, achievements, unlocked packs) and your settings (language, theme, sound, vibration, reminder) are stored only on your device. If you have Google backup turned on, Android may include this data in your Google backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one, for example to reveal a letter, to get another chance, or to spin the wheel again. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (derived from your IP address), and information about how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Learn more in Google's advertising policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings under Google, then Ads.

New films

To add new films without an app update, the app downloads a list of films from a public file hosted on GitHub. This request can reveal your IP address to GitHub, as any web request does. No information about you or your game is sent.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can remind you once a day at 7 pm to play. You can turn this off in the app's Settings or in Android's notification settings.

Sharing

The share buttons open Android's share sheet. What you share goes to the app you choose. We do not receive it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to delete on our side. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings removes the data stored on your phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
