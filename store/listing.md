## Full description (English, max 4000 characters)
Love Bollywood? Prove it.

Filmi Paheli is a guess-the-film puzzle for people who know their cinema. Every puzzle gives you emojis and a clue. Tap the letters to fill in the blanks and name the film before you run out of hearts.

WHAT YOU GET
• 500+ Bollywood films, from black and white classics to the latest hits
• 15 packs: Classics, 70s and 80s, 90s, 2000s, 2010s, Latest Hits, Shah Rukh Khan, Aamir Khan, Salman Khan, Amitabh Bachchan, Akshay Kumar, Leading Ladies, Emoji Round, and the Dialogues and Directors packs that you unlock with coins
• Clues in English or Hindi. Switch any time in Settings
• A new daily puzzle, with a streak to keep alive
• Spin the wheel every day for free coins
• Daily login bonus, and 14 achievements to earn
• Light, dark or system theme
• Sound effects and vibration you can switch off
• Share a clue with a friend and ask them for help
• New films are added regularly, with no update needed

HOW IT WORKS
Look at the emoji and the clue. Tap letters on the keyboard. Each wrong guess costs a heart. You have 3 hearts. Stuck? Spend coins, or watch a short ad, to reveal a letter. Run out of hearts? Watch a short ad for another chance. You earn up to 3 stars and coins for every film you solve.

Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one.

Guess the film. Beat the streak. Become the Bollywood expert.

Filmi Paheli is an independent fan quiz game. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any film studio, actor or director. All film titles belong to their owners and are used only to identify the films.
